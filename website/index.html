Built-in review and access

A finding has a source.
Sharing has a decision.

Sessions record agent activity. Capture turns selected evidence into proposed learnings. Policy decides whether a change can apply or needs a person's review.

Cedar checks each read and write. PostgreSQL row-level security backs tenant isolation, and an audit chain records decisions without copying knowledge bodies into the log.
